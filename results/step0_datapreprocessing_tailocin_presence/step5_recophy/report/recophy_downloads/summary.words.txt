Among 1378 ATUE5 genome pairs, the mean clonal fraction was 0.108 ± 0.195 (median = 0.048). On average, 96% ± 16.3% of SNPs were introduced by recombination (median = 99.3%). Notably, 1318 pairs (95.6%) showed log₁₀(Recombined/Clonal SNPs) > 1, indicating recombination generated more than tenfold the number of clonal SNPs. This highlights pervasive horizontal gene transfer in ATUE5, yet HTF–O-antigen linkage remains fully conserved.
