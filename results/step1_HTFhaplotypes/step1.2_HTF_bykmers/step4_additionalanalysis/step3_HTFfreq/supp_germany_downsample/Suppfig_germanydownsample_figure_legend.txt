Supplementary Fig. X. Downsampling analysis of OBC frequency in German historical and modern strains.
(A) Frequency of OBC− and OBC+ in German historical isolates (n = 8). Points indicate observed proportions and error bars show Wilson 95% confidence intervals. The exact binomial test gives p = 0.289 which is not significant.
(B) To control for differences in sample size and geographic origin, modern isolates from Germany (n = 1,312) were downsampled to n = 8 without replacement across 1,000 runs. Each panel represents one downsampling outcome defined by OBC− count over total and is labeled by its frequency across runs. Points indicate proportions and error bars show Wilson 95% confidence intervals. Background dots represent individual runs.
(C) To evaluate statistical power at n = 8, exact binomial tests were performed for each downsampling run to test deviation from a 50% OBC− frequency. The distribution of p-values is shown and the red dashed line marks α = 0.05. Only 21.9% of runs yield p < 0.05 with a median p of 0.070, indicating limited power and that small samples do not reliably capture the true modern OBC frequency.
